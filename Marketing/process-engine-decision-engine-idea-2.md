# Process Engine Marketing Idea

## Core campaign angle
- Position ILS as more than an LMS: a workflow-aware learning decision engine.
- Most LMS products hardcode status values and stop at reporting.
- ILS connects workflow state, time-based behavior, and intelligence signals into actions.

## Problem framing for sales
- Traditional LMS platforms scatter status logic across tables and pages.
- They offer static dashboards, not operational decision support.
- Schools see risk too late because status history and process context are weak.

## Differentiator statement
- ILS embeds a process engine into education workflows.
- It models real transitions with rules, ownership, and evidence.
- Result: the platform can explain risk and trigger the next best action.

## Value pillars
- Workflow intelligence: statuses are governed transitions, not text labels.
- Operational control: transitions can be restricted by role and readiness conditions.
- Intervention automation: process bottlenecks and declining outcomes drive alerts and follow-up actions.

## High-impact narrative
- Why is a student at risk?
- Time layer: repeated late submissions.
- Process layer: work stuck in ungraded states.
- Intelligence layer: declining outcome mastery.
- Outcome: teacher notified and intervention flow triggered.

## Executive messaging
- This is not another reporting dashboard.
- This is a decision engine for learning operations.
- Schools move from passive visibility to active intervention.

## Suggested campaign lines
- "From status tracking to decision intelligence."
- "Don’t just report risk. Trigger action."
- "The LMS replacement that operationalizes intervention."

## Positioning note
- Legacy systems hardcode status; ILS operationalizes workflow.
- Competitors show what happened; ILS helps decide what to do next.
