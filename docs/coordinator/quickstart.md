# Coordinator Quick Start

This guide explains the daily tasks for coordinators and program managers.

## 1. Dashboards & What to Check
- Open **Coordinator Dashboard** each morning
- Review:
  - Students at-risk list
  - Overdue reviews and progression items
  - Teachers with grading backlog

## 2. Program Scoping
- Filters limit views to your assigned program(s)
- Use the program dropdown to switch context

## 3. Progression Reviews (BSA)
- Open **Progression Reviews** to create or review cases
- Use the timeline to attach evidence and comments
- Final decisions should include a `communicated_at` timestamp before students see them

## 4. Audit & Reports
- Use Audit Logs to export actions for compliance
- Typical exports: grade changes, BSA decisions, attendance edits

## Quick link: Grades export
- For quick exports of grades for your programme, open the Data Exchange and go to the **Grades** tab: [Data Exchange Grades](../admin/data-exchange.md#grades)

## Troubleshooting
- If you see classes outside your program, refresh the page and verify your program assignment in `My Profile` or contact an admin
