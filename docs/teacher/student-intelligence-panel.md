# Student Intelligence Panel — Outcome visibility

Quick, practical guidance for reading outcome-level diagnostics in the Student Intelligence Panel.

## What to look for

- The panel shows the student's overall grade and a breakdown of outcome mastery scores.
 - Any outcome below the diagnostic threshold (recommended: 4.0 / 10) is highlighted in red, even when the overall grade is passing.

## Quick wins (UI guidance)

1. Color-code outcome rows: red <4.0, amber 4.0–6.9, green ≥7.0.
2. Show a short tooltip: "Outcome below threshold — consider targeted remediation." 
3. Add a link to the student's Progression Review for action planning.

## Teacher action

When you see a red outcome while the overall grade is passing, open the Progression Review and add a remediation plan for that outcome.
