# Classroom Intelligence

Classroom Intelligence is the interpretive report that helps you make teaching decisions based on your class's learning evidence.

## What Classroom Intelligence does

It turns the class mastery data into a teacher-ready summary that highlights:
- How many students have enough graded evidence
- How many are below the target threshold
- Whether the class is ready for cohort-level recommendations  
- Whether the class shows patterns (polarized, technical gaps, at-risk clusters)

## How to use it

1. **Go to Classroom Intelligence**
2. **Select your class** from the dropdown
3. **(Optional) Filter by module** if you want to focus on a specific topic
4. **Review the report** — it shows three key sections:

## Section 1: Outcome Breakdown (Weak Areas)

This table shows which outcomes are struggling most, ranked by urgency:

- **Outcome** — the outcome code and name
- **Class Average** — average mastery score
- **% Below Threshold** — percentage of students not yet at the target level
- **Status** — Critical / Attention / Watch / On Track

**What to do**: Focus re-teaching on the Critical and Attention outcomes first.

## Section 2: Cohort Health

A snapshot of your class:
- Total students  
- Students graded
- Students below threshold
- **Re-teach Decision** — does the class need to pause and re-teach?
- Confidence level — how complete is the grading data?

### Understanding the Re-teach Decision

If it says **"Re-teach: Required"**, your class has crossed the policy threshold for students below the target. You should:

1. **Pause progression** to new content
2. **Re-teach** the weak outcomes (use Outcome Breakdown to prioritize)
3. **Re-assess** with a short formative check
4. **Review again** to confirm improvement

If it says **"Re-teach: Not required"**, the class is on track and ready to move forward.

**Key rule**: Only graded students count. If you have 15 students but only 4 are graded, the calculation uses those 4 to determine re-teach status.

## Section 3: Pattern Detection

The report looks for patterns like:

- **Polarized cohort** — large gap between strong and weak students
- **Technical weakness** — technical outcomes lagging behind others
- **Active at-risk** — students below threshold still trying

These patterns help you choose the right intervention.

## Section 4: Suggested Interventions

Based on what you see, the report suggests actions:
- Focused workshops on weak outcomes
- Peer-supported group work  
- Formative reassessment
- Maintaining current pacing (if no issues)

## When to use Classroom Intelligence

- **After the Heatmap** — when you see broad outcome gaps
- **When near re-teach warning** — to confirm it's time to pause
- **Before major curriculum decisions** — to ensure the evidence supports your choice
- **For intervention planning** — to target the right students and outcomes

## How it connects to other views

- **Student Cockpit** → shows what each student needs to do next
- **Heatmap** → shows outcome mastery across the class
- **Classroom Intelligence** → interprets the signal and recommends action
- **Grading** → provides the evidence for the report
- **Interventions** → helps you act on the recommendation

## Tip: Enable "Explain Decisions"

Toggle **"Explain Decisions"** at the top to see the evidence and thresholds behind each recommendation. This helps you understand why the system is flagging something.
