# Criteria Bank

The Criteria Bank provides a read-only review of all rubric criteria across the curriculum.

## What you can do

Go to **Criteria Bank** in the admin menu to:

- View all rubric criteria grouped by rubric and outcome
- Identify duplicate or similar criteria
- Verify which outcomes are linked to criteria
- Scan the full criteria library for consistency

## Workflow

1. Open Criteria Bank
2. Review the criteria library for gaps and overlaps
3. Confirm that rubric criteria are aligned to outcomes

## How it helps

- Review surface for auditing rubric coverage (not an editor)
- Useful during rubric design and curriculum alignment checks
- Helps maintain consistency across rubrics
