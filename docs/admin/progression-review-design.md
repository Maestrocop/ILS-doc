# Progression Review

Progression reviews document student progress and support decisions. The review process tracks two types of information:

## Timeline & Evidence (Past)

Records of support and formal notifications:

- Development meetings — prove support was offered
- Warnings issued — prove formal notification of risk
- Follow-up meetings — document continued support
- Decision meetings — document student hearing
- Student acknowledgments — student confirmed receipt of warning
- Counselor referrals — referral when personal circumstances claimed
- Deferral notifications — opschorting approval when applicable

## Improvement Plan & Actions (Future)

Planned work the student must undertake:

- Planned meetings — visible to student
- Submit assignment — visible to student
- Improve score target — visible to student
- Counselor appointment — visible to student
- Internal coordinator tasks — hidden from student by default (use visibility toggle to reveal)

## Decision Flow

Progression reviews follow a three-stage process:

1. **Mentor Recommendation** — mentor submits recommendation (positive / negative / defer)
2. **Committee Review** — coordinator/admin reviews evidence and prepares decision
3. **Final Decision** — binding decision issued with appeal window

## Key Requirements

Before a negative decision, the system checks:

- ✓ Warning issued
- ✓ Development meeting held
- ✓ Student informed
- ✓ Decision meeting completed
- ✓ Deferral eligibility checked

Each institution can configure these requirements based on local policy.
