# Account & Password Management

This guide covers managing your ILS account, changing passwords, configuring security, and accessing settings.

## Getting Started with Your Account

### First Login

When you first log in to ILS, you'll be prompted to:
1. **Set a secure password** (if using a temporary one)
2. **Verify your email** (click the link sent to your email)
3. **Configure your profile** (add your photo, timezone, language preference)

---

## Password Management

### Password Requirements

Your password must be:
- ✅ **At least 12 characters long**
- ✅ **Include uppercase letters** (A–Z)
- ✅ **Include lowercase letters** (a–z)
- ✅ **Include numbers** (0–9)
- ✅ **Include special characters** (!@#$%^&*)

**Example of a strong password:** `BlueMoon2024!Aug`

### Setting a Strong Password

**Recommendations:**
- Use a passphrase (e.g., "My-Dog-Loves-Pizza-2024")
- Avoid common words, keyboard patterns, or sequences
- Don't reuse passwords from other services
- Don't include your name, username, or student ID
- Change your password annually

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Settings** (click your profile icon)
2. Select **Security** → **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password (twice to confirm)
5. Click **Save**

You'll be logged out after changing your password. Log in with your new password.

### Forgot Your Password?

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a password reset link (valid for 15 minutes)
4. Click the link
5. Enter your new password
6. Click **Reset Password**

**Didn't receive the email?**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Wait 5 minutes and try again
- Contact your school's IT support if the link expires

## Account Security Settings

### View Connected Sessions

See all the devices and locations where you're logged in.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Security**
2. Under **Active Sessions**, you'll see:
   - Device type (computer, phone, browser)
   - Last activity time
   - IP address (approximate location)

### Log Out of Other Sessions

If you see an unrecognized session:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Security** → **Active Sessions**
2. Click **Log Out** next to the suspicious session
3. That device will be logged out immediately
4. If it's your own device, you'll need to log in again

**Do this if:**
- Someone else logs in with your password
- You use a shared computer and forget to log out
- You suspect a security breach

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

**Note:** Two-factor authentication is currently optional for most users. Administrators and coordinators are encouraged to enable it.

**If available, to enable 2FA:**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Security** → **Two-Factor Authentication**
2. Click **Enable 2FA**
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy)
4. Enter the 6-digit code shown in your app
5. Save backup codes in a safe place
6. Confirm

**After enabling 2FA:**
- Every login will ask for your password + 6-digit code from your authenticator app
- If you lose your phone, use one of your backup codes instead

## Profile Settings

### Update Your Profile

1. Go to **Settings** → **Profile**
2. Edit your information:
   - **Name** — verify it's spelled correctly
   - **Email** — your school or personal email
   - **Phone** (optional)
   - **Profile Photo** — upload a professional photo or avatar
   - **Bio** (optional) — brief introduction for teachers (students) or advisors (coordinators)

3. Click **Save**

### Timezone & Preferences

1. Go to **Settings** → **Preferences**
2. Select your timezone (important for when deadlines are displayed)
3. Choose your language (if available)
4. Choose 24-hour or 12-hour time format
5. Click **Save**

### Calendar Integration

If your school supports it, you can integrate ILS with your personal calendar:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Calendar**
2. Click **Connect Calendar**
3. Choose your calendar service (Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.)
4. Authorize ILS to add events
5. ILS will add:
   - Assignment due dates
   - Class sessions
   - Exam dates
   - Custom events you create in ILS

## Notifications & Communication

### Email Notifications

1. Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**
2. Choose which events trigger emails:
   - ✅ **Grades Posted** — when a teacher grades your submission
   - ✅ **New Assignment** — when a teacher publishes a new assignment
   - ✅ **Attendance Alert** — when you're marked absent unexpectedly
   - ✅ **Administrative Notices** — from your school
   - ❓ **Behavioral Alerts** — optional, if your school has enabled it

3. Choose delivery frequency:
   - Immediate (email as events happen)
   - Daily digest (one email per day with all events)
   - Weekly digest (one email per week)

4. Click **Save**

### In-App Notifications

In-app notifications appear in the notification bell (🔔) at the top right.

To manage in-app notifications:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**
2. Toggle **In-App Notifications** on/off

## Account Privacy

### Who Can See My Profile?

**Teachers & Coordinators can see:**
- Your name, email, student/staff ID
- Your grades and submissions (relevant to your class)
- Your attendance

**Other Students can see:**
- Only your name and profile photo (if you've uploaded one)
- NOT your grades, submissions, or attendance

**Administrators can see:**
- Your complete profile and data

### Private vs. Public Information

**Private (shared only with teachers/coordinators/admins):**
- Student ID
- Email address
- Home address (if provided)
- Phone number
- Attendance records
- Submission files

**Public (visible to other students, as needed):**
- Name
- Profile photo
- Class/cohort name

### What Information Can I Keep Private?

1. Go to **Settings** → **Privacy**
2. Toggle these options:
   - **Show my profile photo** — visible to other students (off: only teachers see it)
   - **Show my bio** — visible on my profile page (off: hidden)
   - **Allow file sharing** — other students can download my submissions if I share them (off: no one can access my files)

## Deactivating or Deleting Your Account

### Deactivating Your Account (Temporary)

Temporarily pause your account without deleting data:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**
2. Click **Deactivate Account**
3. Confirm the action
4. Your account will be hidden from the system
5. You can reactivate it anytime by logging back in

### Deleting Your Account (Permanent)

Permanently delete your account and associated data:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**
2. Click **Delete Account**
3. Enter your password to confirm
4. Read the warnings carefully
5. Click **Permanently Delete**

**Important:**
- This action cannot be undone
- Some data (grades, BSA decisions) may be retained for legal/accreditation reasons
- Your school's data protection officer may refuse if retention is required

**After deletion:**
- You cannot log in
- Your profile is removed
- Your submissions remain in the system for recordkeeping
- Your school may still contact you for official communication

## Troubleshooting

### I Can't Log In

**Check:**
1. Is CAPS LOCK on? Passwords are case-sensitive
2. Are you using the correct email address?
3. Is your account active? (admin may have deactivated it)
4. Try resetting your password (see "Forgot Your Password?" above)

**Still having issues?** Contact your school's IT support.

### I Can't Change My Password

**Possible reasons:**
1. Your old password is incorrect
2. Your new password doesn't meet requirements (see Password Requirements above)
3. Your account is locked (too many failed attempts)

**Solution:**
- Use "Forgot Password?" to reset
- Or contact your school's IT support

### I Lost My Phone (with 2FA)

If you've enabled two-factor authentication and lost your phone:

1. Use one of your backup codes (saved when you set up 2FA)
2. Go to **Settings** → **Security** → **Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Click **Disable 2FA**
4. Re-enable it on a new device

**Lost your backup codes?** Contact your school's IT support to verify your identity and reset 2FA.

### I See a Session I Don't Recognize

This could be:
- A device you use occasionally (computer at school, shared laptop)
- An old session that didn't log out automatically
- A security issue

**To be safe:**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Security** → **Active Sessions**
2. Click **Log Out** for unrecognized sessions
3. Change your password
4. If you see the same unrecognized session again, contact IT support

## Security Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Use a unique password for ILS (don't reuse passwords)
- Change your password if you share a computer
- Log out when you leave a computer
- Use a password manager to store strong passwords
- Report suspicious activity to IT support

❌ **DON'T:**
- Share your login credentials with anyone
- Write your password down
- Use a password that's the same across multiple services
- Leave your computer unlocked and unattended
- Click links in suspicious emails claiming to be from ILS
