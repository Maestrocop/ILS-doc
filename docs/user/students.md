# Student Guide

Quick start
- Sign in and open `My Classes` to see current modules and assignments.

Submitting work
- Open the assignment, click `Submit`, attach files, and confirm submission.
- Resubmissions: if allowed, use `Resubmit` and provide updated files.

Viewing grades & feedback
- Check `Gradebook` or `My Progress` for grades, rubric breakdowns, and teacher comments.
- Downloadable feedback: teachers may attach annotated files.

Progress snapshots
- The `Progress` dashboard shows derived outcome snapshots and heatmaps. Use filters to view by module or outcome.

Help & Support
- If you cannot submit, contact your teacher or coordinator. For account issues, see Internal > Account & Password.
