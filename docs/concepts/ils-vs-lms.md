# ILS vs LMS

The Intelligent Learning System (ILS) is not a traditional learning management system (LMS).

## LMS focus

- An LMS focuses on course delivery, enrollment, content organization, and administrative workflows.
- It manages classes, resources, assignments, grades, and student access.
- Typical LMS value is in publishing content and tracking completion.

## ILS focus

- ILS is focused on learning intelligence, measurement, and intervention.
- It tracks outcomes, mastery scores, trends, and at-risk students.
- The platform is built to help teachers interpret student progress and prioritize instruction.

## How they differ

- **LMS = delivery + administration**
  - Content publishing
  - Enrollment and course structure
  - Gradebook and attendance

- **ILS = measurement + insight**
  - Outcome-based mastery
  - Snapshot histories and trends
  - Teacher cockpit and heatmap intelligence

## Where they connect

ILS can sit alongside or on top of an LMS-style curriculum structure: the LMS provides the learning context, while ILS provides the analytics and decision support.
