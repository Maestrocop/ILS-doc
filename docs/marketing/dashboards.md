# Dashboards & Cockpits — Feature Highlight

## Overview

ILS provides role-tailored dashboards: a personal dashboard for students and a cockpit for teachers and coordinators to prioritize interventions and monitor class health.

## Student-facing

My Dashboard surfaces:
- Overall grade and quick stats (submissions, pending, graded)
- Recent grades with feedback links
- Learning outcome performance so students know which skills to focus on

Students can immediately see what needs their attention and get guidance on next steps.

## Teacher-facing

The Teacher Cockpit aggregates:
- Class health pulse
- At-risk students with actionable reasons
- Action inbox for grading and follow-ups
- Class-level learning trends

Teachers move from reactive to proactive: identify risks early, reduce grading bottlenecks, and close outcome gaps with data-driven priorities.

## Why this matters to schools

Role-tailored dashboards help each user focus on what matters most, reducing information overload and enabling faster, more informed decisions.
