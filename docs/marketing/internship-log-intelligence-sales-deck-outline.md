# Internship Log Intelligence — Sales Deck Outline

This outline converts the Internship Log Intelligence concept into a practical sales conversation for school leadership teams.

## Slide 1 — Title and promise

- Title: Talent Demand Index
- Subtitle: Turn internship execution into labor-market intelligence
- Core promise: Evidence-based curriculum adaptation from real workplace activity

## Slide 2 — The institutional problem

- Internship data is collected but underused
- Curriculum updates are often periodic and assumption-driven
- Schools lack a continuous signal of changing company demand

Message to deliver:
Without a market feedback loop, schools risk teaching yesterday's priorities.

## Slide 3 — Why existing approaches are insufficient

- Operational dashboards show completion, not strategic skill demand
- Feedback loops are manual and delayed
- Company signals are fragmented across emails, reports, and local tools

Message to deliver:
Most systems track internship administration, not internship intelligence.

## Slide 4 — The ILS insight

- Activity logs are real-world evidence of student work
- Aggregating logs across cohorts reveals trend signals
- Trend signals can be mapped to curriculum outcomes and modules
- Demand signals should be verified at three levels: observed, repeated, evaluated

Message to deliver:
Internship logs are a live labor-market sensor.

## Slide 5 — What schools can see

- Company trend direction by period and partner
- Most frequent student activities by domain
- Emerging activities not covered well in current curriculum
- Coverage gaps between taught competencies and workplace practice

Message to deliver:
Schools can see where market demand is moving before curriculum review cycles.

## Slide 6 — Academic and strategic value

- Faster curriculum alignment to real demand
- Better employability alignment for graduates
- Stronger evidence for accreditation and quality assurance
- Clear governance narrative for leadership and boards

Message to deliver:
This is both a teaching-quality lever and a strategic planning asset.

## Slide 7 — Example decision workflow

1. Detect rising internship activity cluster
2. Compare against current module/outcome coverage
3. Identify underrepresented competencies
4. Propose targeted curriculum adjustments
5. Re-measure in the next internship cycle

Message to deliver:
ILS enables a repeatable improvement loop, not one-off analysis.

## Slide 8 — Measurable KPIs

- Time from market signal detection to curriculum action
- Percentage of high-frequency internship activities covered in curriculum
- Internship partner outcome quality trend
- Graduate readiness indicators linked to internship evidence
- Skill readiness by sector
- Commercially normalized demand clusters by taxonomy

Message to deliver:
Leadership can monitor progress with concrete, repeatable indicators.

## Slide 9 — Implementation posture

- Start with existing internship log data already captured in ILS
- Use phased rollout: baseline, trend detection, curriculum mapping
- Establish periodic review cadence with curriculum teams
- Keep the student log form light: a few clicks, short note, optional tags

Message to deliver:
No disruptive platform replacement required; intelligence builds on existing workflows.

## Slide 10 — Call to action

- Pilot with one program and one internship cycle
- Define baseline activity taxonomy and review metrics
- Present first market-gap insight report to curriculum board

Message to deliver:
Begin small, prove value quickly, then scale across programs.
