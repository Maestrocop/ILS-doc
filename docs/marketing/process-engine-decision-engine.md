# Process Engine as Decision Engine

## Core campaign angle

- Position ILS as more than an LMS: a workflow-aware learning decision engine.
- Most LMS products hardcode status values and stop at reporting.
- ILS connects workflow state, time behavior, and intelligence signals to trigger action.

## Problem framing

- Legacy LMS status logic is spread across tables and screens.
- Dashboards show status but do not drive intervention.
- Schools discover risk late because process state and history are weak.

## Differentiator

- ILS embeds a process engine in learning workflows.
- Transitions can be governed by role ownership and readiness conditions.
- The platform explains risk and supports intervention actions.

## High-impact narrative

Why is this student at risk?

- Time layer: multiple late submissions.
- Process layer: work stuck in submitted state without grading.
- Intelligence layer: declining mastery trend in outcomes.

Result: teacher notification and intervention workflow can be triggered.

## Campaign lines

- "From status tracking to decision intelligence."
- "Don’t just report risk. Trigger action."
- "The LMS replacement that operationalizes intervention."
