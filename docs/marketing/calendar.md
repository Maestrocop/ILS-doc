# Unified Calendar — Feature Highlight

## What it is

The ILS calendar aggregates all schedule data for students, teachers, and administrators in one place: assignment deadlines and open dates, quiz and exam schedules, teacher-created events, and institution holidays.

## Core capabilities

- **Computed dates**: Assignment and quiz deadlines are automatically included based on what you set in the curriculum
- **Custom events**: Teachers can create events with optional recurrence for class activities or meetings
- **Academic calendar**: Institution-wide events (holidays, exam periods, semester boundaries) are centrally managed and visible to everyone

## Why it matters

A single source of schedule truth for students and staff reduces missed deadlines, eliminates duplicated scheduling efforts, and improves time management across the school.
