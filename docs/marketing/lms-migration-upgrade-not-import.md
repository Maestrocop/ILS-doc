# LMS Migration: Upgrade, Not Import

## Core positioning

- Migrate legacy LMS data into ILS as an upgrade, not a straight import.
- Traditional LMS platforms are content-driven and siloed.
- ILS is a relational learning system with cross-course intelligence.

## Differentiators

- Most migration projects stop at structure or content; ILS extends into intelligence.
- ILS unlocks outcomes, rubrics, competencies, and analytics.
- The process is interpretation and transformation, not copy-paste transfer.

## Sales message

- Migrate from Moodle, Canvas, CSV, and similar systems.
- Use staged migration: structure first, then content, then intelligence.
- Turn messy legacy records into meaningful learning signals.

## Pain points to highlight

- Outcomes are inconsistent across LMS platforms.
- Rubric quality and coverage are often incomplete.
- Grading logic varies significantly and is hard to normalize.

## Positioning line

"Move legacy LMS content into ILS with a migration that preserves structure, imports content, and elevates intelligence."
