# Tracking Your Progress

This page explains how outcome mastery, trends, and confidence work in ILS.

## What mastery means

- Mastery is a normalized score for a learning outcome.
- It is based on graded submissions and rubric evidence.
- A higher mastery score means stronger performance on that learning goal.

## What outcomes are

- Outcomes are learning goals or competencies.
- Each outcome may be linked to multiple assignments and rubric criteria.
- Your progress is tracked separately for each outcome.

## Sample progress view

| Outcome | Mastery | Trend | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra reasoning | 8.0 | Up | High |
| Data interpretation | 6.2 | Flat | Medium |
| Argument writing | 5.0 | Down | Low |

## How to read your progress

- **Up + High confidence**: keep doing what worked.
- **Flat + Medium confidence**: reinforce the outcome with targeted practice.
- **Down + Low confidence**: review recent feedback and ask your teacher for help.

## Using the progress report

- Look for outcomes where your mastery is improving.
- Review trends to see whether your performance is rising or falling.
- Use outcome feedback to know what to focus on next.

## Practical advice

- If a trend is down, ask your teacher for specific guidance.
- If confidence is low, keep working on the same outcome to build consistency.
- Focus on the lowest outcomes first, then strengthen the highest ones.

## Example student action plan

- Identify one outcome with `Low` mastery.
- Review teacher feedback for that outcome.
- Set a specific goal for the next assignment.
- Check your progress again after the next snapshot.
