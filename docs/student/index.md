## How to use this guide

Start with Submitting Work to understand how the platform records your assignment.
Then review Tracking Your Progress to see how outcome mastery is updated when work is graded.
Finally, use Viewing Feedback & Grades to apply teacher comments to your next assignment.

Use Calendar daily to track assignment/quiz deadlines and upcoming class-level events.


## Why this matters

Knowing these signals helps you take ownership of your learning, respond to teacher feedback, and focus on the outcomes that matter most.
