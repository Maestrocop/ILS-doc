# Student Quick Start

This short guide helps students use the main classroom features in ILS.

## 1. Today & Calendar
- Open **Calendar** to see your schedule for the week.
- The dashboard shows upcoming sessions and due dates.
- Click a session to see the module, teacher, room, and related assignments.

## 2. Submitting Work
1. Go to **Submissions** or the assignment link in your class
2. Click **Upload** or **Start Submission**
3. Add files and optional comments
4. Click **Submit**
5. You can re-submit if the teacher allows it

## 3. Viewing Grades & Feedback
- Open **Dashboard** → **Recent Grades** to see scores
- Click a grade to open the submission and read teacher feedback (if available)

## 4. Managing Notifications
- Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** to choose email or in-app alerts
- Recommended: enable assignment and grade notifications

## 5. Exporting Your Data
- Go to **Settings** → **Privacy & Data** → **Request Export**
- You’ll receive an email with your data within 24 hours

## Troubleshooting
- Missing session on calendar: check the Timetable page or contact your teacher
- Can't find feedback: open the submission from Recent Grades or contact your teacher
