# Student Submission Workflow

This guide explains how students submit work, view feedback, and track resubmissions.

## Submitting an Assignment

1. Open **Assignments** or navigate to the course page and open the assignment card.
2. Click **Submit work** and attach files (PDF, DOCX, images) or enter text directly if the assignment supports it.
3. Add a short note for your teacher if helpful.
4. Click **Submit**. You will see a confirmation and a timestamp.

## Versioning & Resubmissions

- After submitting, you may be allowed to resubmit depending on teacher settings. Check the assignment instructions for resubmission policy.
- Each submission is timestamped; teachers may grade a single final submission or multiple versions.

## Viewing Feedback and Grades

1. Go to your `Submissions` list to find graded work.
2. Open the submission to view rubric scores, grader comments, and attached feedback files.
3. If the teacher returned the submission for revision, follow the comments and resubmit as instructed.

## Troubleshooting

- "My file failed to upload": try a different browser or compress the file. If problems persist, download and attach a PDF.
- "I don't see feedback": ensure your teacher has finalized the grade; drafts are not visible to students.
- "I need an extension": contact your teacher directly via the messaging or email provided in the class page.
